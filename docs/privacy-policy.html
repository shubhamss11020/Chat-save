Chat-Save Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Before publishing: replace every text in square brackets, and have a responsible person at [COMPANY] review this page.

1. What Chat-Save is

Chat-Save is a browser extension used by [COMPANY] employees. It saves the conversations people have with Claude on claude.ai to [COMPANY]'s internal archive.

2. What we collect

  • The messages in your Claude conversations, including both your prompts and Claude's replies.
  • Files you attach to those conversations, and files Claude creates in them.
  • The title, creation time and last-update time of each conversation.
  • Your Chat-Save access key, which identifies you to the server, and the date you agreed to saving.

Chat-Save does not read anything outside claude.ai, and does not collect your browsing history, passwords, or other websites' data.

3. How your consent works

Nothing is saved until you read the welcome page and choose Start saving. The extension shows a label on claude.ai while saving is on. You can stop saving at any time in the Chat-Save settings, and you can withdraw your consent there as well.

4. Where the data goes

  • Conversations are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to the Chat-Save server operated by [COMPANY] and hosted on Render.
  • They are stored in a private PostgreSQL database and a private file storage bucket, both operated by [COMPANY] through [STORAGE PROVIDER].
  • A readable copy is kept in a private Git repository controlled by [COMPANY].

Data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone outside [COMPANY], except the hosting and storage providers named above.

5. Who can access it

  • Each person can see only their own saved conversations.
  • The [COMPANY] administrator can see all saved conversations, to run and maintain the archive.
  • Access is limited by individual access keys, which can be cancelled at any time.

6. How long we keep it

Saved conversations are kept for [RETENTION PERIOD], then deleted. You can ask for earlier deletion at any time (see section 8).

7. Security

Connections use HTTPS. Access keys are private to each person, and are never placed in web addresses. Storage is private and encrypted by its provider.

8. Your rights and how to contact us

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the conversations saved about you, and to stop saving at any time. Email [CONTACT EMAIL]. We reply within [X] working days.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how Chat-Save works, we will update this page and ask you to agree again before saving continues.
