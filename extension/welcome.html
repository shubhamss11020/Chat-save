Chat-Save will save your Claude chats

Please read this before you continue.

What is saved

  • Every conversation you have on claude.ai, including messages and files you attach.
  • Chats from the Claude web, desktop and phone apps on your account.

Where it goes

  • Sent over an encrypted connection to the Chat-Save server.
  • Stored there in a private database and private file storage.
  • Only the administrator can see every chat. You can see only your own.

Your choices

  • Nothing is saved until you press Start saving below.
  • You can pause saving at any time in the Chat-Save settings.
  • A label on claude.ai shows whether saving is on.
