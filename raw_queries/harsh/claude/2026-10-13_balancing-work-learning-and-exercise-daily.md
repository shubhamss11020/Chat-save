Here's a routine you can adapt. It's built around energy levels: hard thinking early, movement in the middle, and learning when your attention is fresh but not frantic.

## Daily Routine (Weekday Template)

| Time | Block | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 6:30 | Wake and reset | Water, light, 5 minutes of stretching, no phone |
| 7:00 | **Exercise (30-45 min)** | Strength, running, or cycling; alternate days |
| 7:45 | Breakfast and plan | Pick your top 3 priorities for the day |
| 8:30 | **Deep work #1 (90 min)** | Your hardest, most important task |
| 10:00 | Break (15 min) | Walk, no screens |
| 10:15 | **Deep work #2 (60-90 min)** | Continue the priority work |
| 12:00 | Lunch and a short walk | Away from your desk |
| 1:00 | **Shallow work (2 hrs)** | Email, meetings, admin, quick tasks |
| 3:00 | Break (15 min) | Snack, stretch |
| 3:15 | **Learning block (45-60 min)** | Course, book, or skill practice |
| 4:15 | Wrap-up (15 min) | Review the day, set up tomorrow |
| 4:30 | Done with work | Hard stop |
| Evening | Free time | Dinner, relationships, hobbies |
| 9:30 | Wind down | Dim lights, read, no screens |
| 10:30 | Sleep | Aim for 7-8 hours |

## Why it's structured this way

- **Deep work goes first.** Willpower and focus are highest in the morning, so your most demanding task gets that slot, before email and meetings take over.
- **Exercise comes before work.** Morning exercise improves focus for hours afterward and is harder to skip than evening exercise, which gets bumped when the day runs long.
- **Learning has its own protected block.** Putting it in the afternoon, after the deep work, keeps it from being squeezed out by urgent tasks. If you have more energy in the evening, swap it there.
- **Breaks are scheduled.** Short breaks between focus blocks prevent the late-afternoon crash.

## Weekly rhythm for exercise

- **Mon / Wed / Fri:** strength training (30-45 min)
- **Tue / Thu:** cardio, such as a run, bike, or brisk walk (30 min)
- **Sat:** something fun and active, like a hike, sport, or class
- **Sun:** rest, or light mobility work

## Learning tips

- Pick **one** main skill per quarter rather than five at once.
- Spend about 70% of the time **practicing** (projects, exercises) and 30% consuming (reading, videos).
- Keep a short log of what you learned each day. Two sentences is enough.

## Make it stick

1. **Start small.** Adopt two or three blocks first, such as the morning workout and one deep-work session, then add more over a few weeks.
2. **Protect the blocks.** Put them on your calendar as real appointments.
3. **Use a weekly review.** Spend 15 minutes on Friday afternoon or Sunday evening checking what worked and adjusting.
4. **Plan for bad days.** Define a minimum version, such as a 10-minute workout, 20 minutes of focused work, and 15 minutes of learning, so a rough day doesn't break the streak.

## Adjusting for your situation

- **Night owl:** shift everything about 2 hours later, but keep the order (exercise, deep work, shallow work, learning).
- **Meeting-heavy job:** protect one deep-work block by blocking the time on your calendar, and cluster meetings into the afternoon.
- **Short on time:** shrink to three blocks, which are 20 minutes of exercise, 60 minutes of deep work, and 20 minutes of learning.
