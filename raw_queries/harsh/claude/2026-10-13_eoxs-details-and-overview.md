EOXS (legal entity Prata Inc.) is a vertical SaaS and AI company that sells software to steel and metals distributors. It is headquartered in New Rochelle, NY, and its tagline is "Where Steel Meets Technology."

**Positioning**
- It markets itself as the first AI-native ERP built only for steel and metals distribution. It competes with SAP, Oracle NetSuite and Epicor, arguing generic ERPs can't handle steel's costing, inventory and processing needs.
- The founding team claims 30 years of combined steel experience. The company has been publicly active since about 2021, with phone lines in Houston and Toronto.
- Rajat Jain is the CEO.

**Two product tracks**

| Track | What it includes |
|---|---|
| Core ERP (Odoo-based) | Five modules: Books (accounting and inventory), CRM, People (HR and payroll), Shop (e-commerce) and Report Builder. The "President's Cockpit" puts all KPIs on one executive screen. |
| AI Workers | 12+ task-specific agents. Examples: SmartQuote AI and WiseQuote AI (quoting), Contract Review, Order Review and PO AIs, ViewStock AI and SteelSynk AI (inventory and demand forecasting), RevTrack AI and Iris (customer intelligence), SnapReceive AI (receiving), and Steel Brain AI (metallurgy). |

- **Interoperability:** The AI Workers are marketed as ERP-agnostic. They work with Invera, Stratix, SAP, Epicor and EOXS ERP.
- **Deployment:** Cloud deployments are estimated at under 2 weeks. Complex or on-premise ones take 4 to 6 weeks.
- **Claimed accuracy:** 98%+ on quote generation and 90%+ on price recommendations.
- **Integrations:** 34+ third-party tools.
- **Compliance:** GDPR and CCPA.
- **Trial:** A 15-day free trial with full features.
- **Steel Brain AI:** This is the same product as "AI Joe", built on the knowledge of a Gerdau melt-shop veteran.

**Active clients (8 in the registry)**
3GM Steel, Brannon Steel, Discount Pipe & Steel, Eastern States Steel, Ohio Strip Steel (formerly Greer Steel), PPC Metals, RW Conklin Steel and Sabre Alloys. Most have their own `*.eoxs.com` instance.

**Other activity**
- It publishes thought leadership: the book *Making Steel Sexy Again*, a Stories On Steel video series, a blog, and an EOXS Training Video Library run by Sheenam Rawat's team.
- Rajat attended the SMU Steel Summit 2026 in Atlanta.
- In August 2026 Ayan Dutta announced a company-wide rollout of Cruz, the AI knowledge platform. It is now being built out as AskCruz.
- Before 2021 it was an outsourced-development Odoo shop.
