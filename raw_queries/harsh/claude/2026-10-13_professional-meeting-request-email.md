Here's a draft you can adapt:

**Subject:** Request for a Meeting – [Topic]

Hi [Manager's Name],

I hope you're doing well. I'd like to schedule a time to meet with you to discuss [topic, e.g., my current project priorities / my career development / feedback on recent work].

The meeting should take about [20–30 minutes]. I'm available [day/time options, e.g., Wednesday afternoon or Thursday morning], but I'm happy to work around your schedule. If it's easier, I can send a calendar invite once you let me know what suits you.

Please let me know if there's anything you'd like me to prepare beforehand.

Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Your Title]

A few tips: stating the topic up front helps your manager decide how much time to set aside, and offering two or three specific time slots makes it easier for them to reply.
