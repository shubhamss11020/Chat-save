Here's the 5ft differentiation between 3GM and AskCruz:

3GM Vault (Source Data)
- A single customer's full operational record: 3GM Steel
- 548 wiki pages, 19,765 facts, 24,949 raw emails from Stefan and Travis's inboxes
- Covers: customers (214 pages), suppliers, orders, pricing, disputes, competitive deals
- Primary use: tracking a single company's business (customers, suppliers, coils, freight, deals)
- Data sources: raw Outlook mailboxes (2), all activities cross-cited
- Scale: deep coverage of one entity's ecosystem

AskCruz Vault (Consulting/ERP Implementation Platform)
- A consulting practice managing 8 clients across steel distribution/ERP implementation
- 1,087 wiki pages, 27,306 email threads, 2,102 calls, 777 implementation tasks
- Covers: 8 clients (Sabre Alloys, Eastern States Steel, Greer Steel, PPC Metals, 3GM Steel, Discount Pipe & Steel, Brannon Steel, RW Conklin Steel)
- Primary use: tracking multi-client ERP implementations, onboarding, support, and deployments
- Data sources: email, calls (Fireflies + Fathom), implementation task tracking, client contacts
- Scale: broad coverage across many implementations and clients

Key Difference
3GM vault = Inside-out (one customer's full operational view, their customers/suppliers/deals). AskCruz vault = Outside-in (EOXS consulting view of 8 clients' implementations and deployments). 3GM appears in both—as a subject in 3GM vault and as a client in AskCruz.
