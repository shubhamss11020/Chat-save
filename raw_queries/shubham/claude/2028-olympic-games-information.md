The 2028 Summer Olympics are in Los Angeles, and the opening ceremony is July 14, 2028.

**Dates**
- The Games run from the July 14, 2028 opening ceremony through July 30, 2028.
- The Paralympics start August 15, 2028 and close August 27, 2028.
- This is LA's third time hosting the Olympics and its first time hosting the Paralympics.
- The soccer tournament runs July 10-30, 2028.

**Venues**
- Gymnastics will be at Crypto.com Arena.
- Track and field will be at the LA Memorial Coliseum.
- SoFi Stadium will host swimming, in what officials say will be the largest swimming venue in Olympic history.
- Tennis and track cycling will be in Carson, and weightlifting at the Theatre venue at LA Live.
- Soccer will use seven venues, with both gold medal matches at the Rose Bowl in Pasadena. The other soccer venues include stadiums outside California.
- The first look at the schedule has track and field in the first week and swimming in the second.

**Tickets**
- More than 6 million tickets have been sold, and more than 700,000 fans bought at the $28 level.
- More first-come, first-served sales are planned for 2027, with Paralympic tickets available for the first time that year.
- The official ticketing providers are AXS and CTS Eventim.
- There is a 12-ticket limit for general events, plus a separate 12-ticket limit for soccer.

Ticket phases and prices change, so check the official LA28 site before buying. I can go deeper on any sport or on travel planning if you like.

## Claude (2026-10-12 15:11:45 EDT)

The host for the 2036 Summer Olympics hasn't been chosen yet. It's an open contest, and the winner will be picked by a vote.

**Where the race stands (as of early October 2026)**
- On October 6, 2026, the IOC said seven countries, including India and Qatar, have expressed interest. The deadline for other national Olympic bodies to join is October 31.
- The IOC plans to pick the winner through a contested vote in 2029.
- The IOC executive board will make key decisions over the next 2½ years, guided by a 30-page document the IOC published for bidders.
- In March, the executive board is due to approve a shortlist of candidates to develop detailed projects.
- IOC President Kirsty Coventry paused the fast-track selection process on taking office in June 2025. That process had let Brisbane be picked as the 2032 host 11 years in advance, without a contested vote.

**Bids named so far**
- **India:** The plan centers on Ahmedabad, which hosts the Commonwealth Games in 2030 and has the world's largest cricket stadium.
- **Qatar:** The bid is built around Doha, and it could expand to send some events to Gulf neighbors. A Qatar-hosted Games would likely mean moving the Summer Games out of the July-August slot used at every edition from 2004 through 2032.
- **South Korea:** The bid would be anchored in Seoul, which hosted the 1988 Games, and spread across the country.
- **South Africa:** The bid is national and centered on Cape Town, which would make it the first African host of the Summer Games.

The articles I found name only four of the seven bidders. Earlier interest from Poland and Indonesia (a bid centered on Nusantara, its future capital) was reported in earlier years, and Mexico withdrew its bid, citing tough competition. I can't confirm from these sources which countries make up the seven today, so check the IOC announcement for the full list.
