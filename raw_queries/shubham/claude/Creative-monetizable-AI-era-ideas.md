Here's a creative monetization angle for the AI era:

**AI-Powered "Expert Extractors" for Niche Industries**

Build AI agents that interview domain experts (plumbers, mechanics, small law practices, accountants, etc.) and extract their tacit knowledge into structured, sellable assets. The monetization model would be:

1. **Subscription SaaS for practitioners**: The expert themselves gets a tool that packages their knowledge as:
   - An AI assistant they can license to competitors
   - A decision-tree guide for new staff training
   - A FAQ/troubleshooting chatbot for clients

2. **B2B licensing**: Franchise operators and solo practitioners pay to license "expert models" from recognized masters in their field (a viral plumbing workflow, a proven tax strategy, etc.)

3. **Hybrid human + AI consulting**: Use the extracted knowledge to route complex cases back to the original expert for premium rates, while AI handles 80% of standard queries.

**Why it works in the AI era:**
- Experts capture value they usually lose when they retire
- Young practitioners get institutional knowledge overnight
- Clients get faster, consistent service
- Zero-shot distillation of expertise is finally feasible with modern AI

**Differentiation**: Instead of generic "AI chatbots," you're selling *documented mastery*—the thing enterprises and SMBs actually need but can't easily codify.

This works because it plays to what AI does best (pattern matching at scale) while preserving the human value (judgment, nuance, presence for premium work).
