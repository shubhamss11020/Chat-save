## EOXS Leave Policy

**For Regular Employees:**

- **Allocation:** 5 sick leaves + 5 casual leaves per calendar year
- **Planned leave:** Must be requested via the EOXSTEAM portal at least 48 hours in advance and approved before confirmation
- **Sick leave (>1 day):** Requires a medical certificate within 48 hours of returning
- **Emergency/sudden leave:** Must be communicated immediately to your reporting manager
- **Application:** Via the EOXSTEAM platform (teams.eoxs.com)

**For Interns (Additional Restrictions):**

- **First two weeks:** No leave permitted except urgent situations
- **Allocation:** 5 sick leaves + 5 casual leaves per year (until joining anniversary)
- **If exhausted before one year:** Any additional leave is unpaid; after one year, email HR to request the next year's 10-leave allocation
- **Casual leave:** Requires Team Lead approval at least 2 days in advance, then applied on EOXSTEAM portal
- **Sick leave:** Medical certificate required; apply on EOXSTEAM portal the next working day after returning

**Platform:** All leave is logged on the EOXSTEAM portal (teams.eoxs.com). New employees receive a video walkthrough during onboarding.

**Source:** EOXS Employee Conduct & Policies document (last updated April 2025); Intern Onboarding email from HR (Isha Bisht).
