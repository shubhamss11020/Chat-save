**AskCruz** is an AI product, built by EOXS, that connects a company's scattered business data and lets employees ask questions of it in one place. Its internal wiki describes it as a "Company Brain."

- **Core idea:** Data from email, spreadsheets, operational systems, project tools, and call records is structured into company knowledge, which people can query conversationally. The pipeline runs Connect → Collect → Filter → Classify → Structure → Verify → Approve → Ask.
- **Capabilities:** The product is organized around four named capabilities: Company Brain (shared working knowledge), Company Hands (practical work it can help perform, such as drafting a quote), and two others I didn't reach in the excerpt.
- **Boundaries:** Live source systems stay authoritative. Data is synthesized with about a six-hour lag. Access depends on the user's identity. Draft knowledge isn't treated as final until approved. It explains information but doesn't perform actions in source systems.
- **Freshness of positioning:** Two internal documents disagree. One describes AskCruz as built for steel businesses. The other says it is industry-agnostic. The wiki flags this and says the canonical positioning should be confirmed before external use.
- **Sales status:** It is being pitched to several steel companies. 3GM Steel is the first confirmed customer, at a reduced two-user scope with a shorter initial term (August 2026). Its kickoff invoice is dated September 6, 2026. Sabre Alloys has a proposal under review, and Commerce Steel is a prospect. The wiki says 3GM's usage, retention, and ROI outcomes haven't been verified yet.
- **Internal work:** A development team runs a task board and daily reports, and there is a marketing push through October 12, 2026, which includes a car dealership vertical.

I read only the opening of the product pages, so I can go deeper on any section, the sales pipeline, or the 3GM deal.
