AskCruz (product family name "Cruz") is EOXS's AI knowledge product. It connects a company's scattered business data and turns it into searchable, cited answers through one conversational interface. The sales material calls it "Company Brain."

**How it works**
Data moves through ten stages: source systems, ingestion, filtering, sensitivity classification, a central knowledge store, knowledge generation, citation check, human approval, retrieval, and response. Synthesis runs about every six hours, so answers can lag live systems. Each synthesized page must be approved by a person before it is published.

**What it draws from**
- Gmail (three individual accounts)
- A shared Zoho support inbox
- Fireflies and Fathom call transcripts
- Odoo implementation boards for six clients: Greer Steel, Eastern States Steel, Discount Pipe & Steel, PPC Metals, 3GM Steel and Sabre Alloys. RW Conklin and Brannon Steel appear only through email and calls.

Support tickets, invoices and sales orders are not part of AskCruz's own collection. They stay in EOXS's live Odoo system and are reached through the separate `eoxs-teams` connector.

**Access control**
Access depends on who is asking. Leadership sees confidential and personal material. HR sees confidential material with financial detail restricted. General staff see general business information with financial amounts and employee performance removed. Interns see general information only. Restrictions are enforced at the database level, with an AI-level check layered on top. The system is designed to refuse anything it can't clearly classify as open.

**Product model**
Four capabilities: Company Brain (shared company knowledge), Company Hands (practical tasks such as drafting a quote, with decisions left to people), Digital Workforce (ongoing monitoring of business conditions), and Company Personalization (reflecting one company's own terms, pricing and vocabulary).

**Positioning is unresolved**
The product-feature file describes it as built for steel businesses. The market-fit guide calls it industry-agnostic. The wiki flags this and says the positioning should be confirmed before any external use.

**The 3GM deal**
