AskCruz is an AI product, pitched as a “Company Brain,” that connects a company’s scattered business data (email, spreadsheets, operational systems, project tools, call records) and lets employees ask questions across it through one conversational interface. The product is built around four capabilities: Company Brain (shared company knowledge), Company Hands (practical tasks like drafting a quote, with final decisions left to people), Digital Workforce (standing monitoring of business conditions), and Company Personalization (reflecting one company’s own terms and pricing logic).

Its market positioning is unsettled. The internal product-feature file describes it as built for steel businesses, while the product-market-fit guide calls it industry-agnostic. The wiki flags this contradiction and says the current positioning should be confirmed before any external use.

Traction: 3GM Steel is the first confirmed paying customer. Travis confirmed the deal at a reduced two-user scope and asked for a shorter initial term (Aug 2026). The wiki notes that usage depth, ROI and retention for 3GM have not been verified. Sabre Alloys received a proposal, with follow-up scope discussions in September. Other prospects include Commerce Steel and Three D Metals, and EOXS has proposed a reseller partnership through Invera.

Other details: The project board runs on an internal Odoo instance with about 39 active tasks. Ron’s daily task reports and marketing priorities through Oct 12 are also in the wiki.
