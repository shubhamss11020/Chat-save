## Claude (2026-10-06T00:00:00Z)

AskCruz is an organizational knowledge and information-finding product that brings together relevant business information from available sources through conversational interface. Here's what it does:

## Core capabilities

**Email Information** — Find and understand business correspondence, requests, decisions, agreements from Gmail, Zoho, or other supported email sources.

**Call & Meeting Information** — Access discussions, action items, decisions, and feedback from call and meeting records (Fireflies, Fathom, or other supported sources).

**Client / Implementation Information** — Understand implementation progress, tasks, status, updates, and blockers through Odoo, implementation boards, or other supported systems.

**Employee Information** — Find approved employee, contact, and organizational information from employee directories or HR sources.

**Internal Knowledge & Documents** — Access policies, procedures, SOPs, guides, assets, and reference material.

**Operational Records & Business Transactions** — Retrieve sales orders, invoices, support tickets, and other operational data through live ERP connections or vault ingestion (configuration per client).

**Cross-Source Context** — Combine relevant information from multiple sources when a broader business question requires it.

## Key product principles

- Capability-driven, not tool-driven. Sources (Gmail, Odoo, etc.) are examples; what matters is whether the capability can address the user's question.
- Client-specific availability. A capability is product-supported but not automatically available to every client; depends on their sources, information availability, and approved access.
- Information-finding, not system replacement. AskCruz surfaces available information but should redirect users to original systems for moment-to-moment operational state or actions.
- Access and freshness constraints. Users see only approved information; data has documented collection lag and may not reflect changes made seconds ago.

Currently EOXS internal (pilot), first external client is 3GM (mid-implementation).
