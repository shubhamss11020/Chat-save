I'll start by reading the two skill files.

AskCruz is the AI product (the "Company Brain") that EOXS sells and runs. It is sold by the same people, to the same customers, and is run from inside the EOXS business, but its legal structure is not clearly documented.

**How they connect**
- **Same people.** Rajat Jain and Ron J run EOXS sales and implementation, and they also pitch and deliver AskCruz. Ron J's October 8 priorities email covers AskCruz marketing, with car dealerships and steel as the first two verticals.
- **Same customers.** The first AskCruz deal was with 3GM Steel, EOXS's oldest ERP client, which has been billed since 2022. A kickoff invoice for the AskCruz implementation went out on Sept 6, 2026, and the deal was confirmed at a reduced 2-user scope. AskCruz is also being walked through to prospects such as Regional Doors & Hardware and Lumberman's Wholesale.
- **Same sales channel.** Those AskCruz walkthroughs are booked on the "External Calls EOXS" calendar.
- **Upsell path.** One prospect page (Collier Metals) describes the AskCruz relationship as a way in that could later lead to the full EOXS platform. That is an interpretation in the page, not a stated strategy.

**Where they differ**
- **Product.** EOXS is Odoo-based ERP implementation and licensing for steel distributors. AskCruz is an AI layer over a company's own emails, calls and documents, and is aimed beyond steel, with car dealerships as the next vertical.
- **Mail domain.** The August 2026 intern cohort got `@askcruz.com` mailboxes, separate from the `eoxs.com` and `eoxsteam.com` domains.
- **Billing entity.** The 3GM AskCruz kickoff invoice was issued by GFC Intelligence, Inc., an entity that appears nowhere else in the record. The wiki flags this as unverified, so I can't say whether AskCruz bills through that entity in general or only for this deal.

**What I couldn't confirm**
- The legal structure between EOXS (Prata Inc.), AskCruz and GFC Intelligence.
- Whether AskCruz and EOXS share an invoice sequence. Both use the same invoice-number format, but the wiki couldn't tell whether that is deliberate.
- Money figures. This connection hides them.
