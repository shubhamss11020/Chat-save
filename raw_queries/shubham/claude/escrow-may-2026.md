In May 2026, escrow appears only in two places, and neither is a 3GM escrow arrangement. The first is trade-newsletter coverage of Worthington Steel's financing for its Kloeckner acquisition. The second is a zero-value escrow line in a 3GM cash flow forecast.

**1. Worthington's $900M escrow financing (Steel Market Update newsletters, May 26 and 28)**
- On May 26, the newsletter reported that Worthington would sell notes through WS Escrow LLC, a wholly owned Worthington subsidiary. It said proceeds may be held in escrow unless the Kloeckner deal is expected to close within three business days of the offering.
- On May 28, the newsletter reported that WS Escrow plans to raise $900 million through senior secured notes to fund the Kloeckner buy. The same issue said all regulatory conditions for the acquisition had been met and that closing would come soon.
- This is a third party's financing, not 3GM's. The newsletter was the only source for these figures, and I didn't check them against any other document.

**2. Escrow line in 3GM's cash flow forecast (Stefan, May 14)**
- The "Cash Flow Forecast - 3GM.xlsx" attachment has a "To reserve and/or escrow" line. It is $0 in every month from May 2026 to April 2027.
