The vault has about 25 documents that track inventory, and several series share one report under different names: the "Available & HFI" files and the "Inventory Update" files are the same kind of snapshot. The newest full snapshot is the 1 Oct 2026 "3GM Inventory Update" workbook. These are dated snapshots, not live stock. For current on-hand inventory, the ERP is the authority.

**Inventory snapshots (Jessica Worley)**
- 3GM Inventory Update - 10.1.2026.xlsx: att 46214, sent 1 Oct 2026 (msg 287870)
- 3GM Inventory Update - 9.11.26.xlsx: att 45676, 11 Sep 2026 (msg 209598)
- 3GM Inventory - 9.22.2026.xlsx: att 45916, 22 Sep 2026 (msg 253889)
- 3GM Inventory Update8.25.2026.xlsx: att 9616, 25 Aug 2026 (msg 47022)

**Available & HFI inventory (same report, different names)**
- September 2026 - Available & HFI Inventory.xlsx: att 46193, sent 1 Oct 2026 (msg 286626). Its subject line is "3GM - Available & HFI".
- August 2026 - Available & HFI Inventory.xlsx: att 9199, sent 1 Sep 2026 (msg 46554)
- Wiki page "3GM - Available & HFI Inventory" (page 7) covers this series.

**Aged inventory clear-out**

**Planning, usage and reject tracking**
- 3GM NEW Inventory Order Planning.xlsx: att 45674 (msg 209480, 11 Sep 2026) and att 9789 (msg 47286, 20 Aug 2026). Wiki page "3GM Inventory Order Planning" (189) describes the process.
- CMT General 90-365 Inventory Usage.xls: att 46304. CMT Main-Hoosier Inventory Value_Usage 112026-9302026.xls: att 46303 (both msg 291514, 2 Oct 2026)

**Process context**
Travis Lane describes the tracking method in a March 2026 Dongkuk thread: a combination of historical burn rate and forward demand projections by product (msg 68545). Wiki page "Inventory Stocking Policy" (75) covers how stocking decisions are made.

**Left out:** the "Custom Built Inventory List" PDFs from Koil Metals are supplier sale offers for coil they are selling, not 3GM's own tracking. Most of the 277 inventory-named attachments are these.
