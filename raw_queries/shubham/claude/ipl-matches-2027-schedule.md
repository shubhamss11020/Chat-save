The Indian Premier League (IPL) and the Big Bash League (BBL) are both franchise T20 leagues, but they differ in scale, money and format.

| | IPL (India) | BBL (Australia) |
|---|---|---|
| **Started** | 2008 | 2011-12 |
| **Season** | Roughly March to May | Roughly December to January/February |
| **Teams** | 10, named for Indian cities or states | 8, named for Australian cities |
| **Matches** | About 74, with a long league stage, playoffs and a final | Far fewer, about 40 league games plus finals |
| **Player selection** | Mega and mini auctions with budget caps, plus retentions | A draft, with salary caps per club |
| **Money and profile** | By far the richest T20 league, with the biggest salaries and broadcast deals | Smaller budgets but a strong summer-holiday audience |
| **Overseas stars** | Many, but only 4 per playing XI | Many, with limits on overseas players per team |
| **Home advantage** | Strong, since teams have their own home grounds | Mostly the same, though a few games are played at neutral or regional venues |

**Other differences:**
- **Who plays:** the IPL pulls in most of the world's top T20 players because of the money, while the BBL's quality depends more on Australian internationals, who are often away on national duty.
- **Atmosphere:** IPL crowds are huge and loud, and the tournament is a major media event. The BBL is more relaxed and family-oriented, often played in an Australian summer evening setting.
- **Rules:** the BBL has trialled several experimental rules (such as special power-play options), and the IPL has its own, like the Impact Player. Both change from season to season, so check the current rulebooks.

If you're picking one to follow, the IPL has more depth and drama, while the BBL is shorter and easier to dip into. Let me know if you'd like a closer comparison of anything, such as rules or the auction versus the draft.
