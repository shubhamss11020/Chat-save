# **EOXS Compensation Strategy — Synthesized from Thread Wiki Record**

## **The Reality**

Your compensation system is **partially documented and structurally inconsistent.** You have:

- ✅ **A ladder** (encoded in offer letters and payslips, not in a single document)
- ✅ **A headcount and monthly spend** (₹RESTRICTED / ~$RESTRICTED Aug 2026 across 80 employees)
- ✅ **Component structure** (Basic + HRA + Conveyance + Allowances + Misc Earning/Bonus)
- ❌ **No written salary grades or promotion criteria**
- ❌ **No rule for the discretionary Misc Earning/Bonus line** (5x swing, no formula)
- ❌ **No AskCruz distinction** (listed as "Cruz" in payroll COMPANY column, but documented as "not separate")
- ❌ **No coverage for ~24 active employees** (mostly interns/VAs—no payroll row at all)
- ❌ **Raises disconnected from contraction strategy** (you're in cost-cutting mode, so raises need explicit defense)

---

## **The Problem This Creates**

1. **Fairness disputes are inevitable.** You cannot defend "Hashir gets ₹X" without a written rule, so when someone comparable asks why they don't, you're stuck.
2. **Interns/VAs disappear.** 24 of 57 active people are invisible to formal systems. Leads to batch-rate errors (Nandita Rana's disbursement override, Aug 2026).
3. **Raises feel arbitrary during contraction.** Your 2025→2026 spend dropped ~RESTRICTED%. A raise in that context needs a stated reason, not just "he asked."
4. **Retention signal is weak.** No written path to higher pay = no reason to stay. Especially for technical people and potential poaches.

---

## **Strategy — Three Tiers**

### **Tier 1: Immediate (Next 2–4 Weeks)**

Document what you already have. **Don't invent new structure yet—codify the actual behavior.**

1. **Extract the ladder from offer letters & signed contracts** (Raj 152, Aug 2026, already has this). Organize it as **Levels L0–L5:**
   - L0: Intern (₹RESTRICTED–RESTRICTED/mo, 1-yr non-compete, 30-day notice)
   - L1: Junior FTE conversion (₹RESTRICTED–RESTRICTED/mo, 5-yr non-compete, ₹RESTRICTED 2-yr bond, ₹RESTRICTED/mo accrual)
   - L2: Mid-level IC (Ron, Aryan range: ~₹RESTRICTED–RESTRICTED/mo)
   - L3: Senior IC (Dhrup contract: ₹RESTRICTED/mo, locked through 2027)
   - L4: Leadership (undefined; not yet documented)
   - L5: (Reserved)

2. **Define the Misc Earning/Bonus rule.** Three options:
   - **Option A (objective):** X% of base, paid per quarter if company revenue or project milestone hit.
   - **Option B (subjective but bounded):** 0–20% discretionary, tied to written performance goals, approved by you quarterly.
   - **Option C (transparent):** Publish the ranges you actually used in 2025 & 2026; explain what triggered each decision.
   
   **Recommendation:** Option B. It's honest, defensible, and doesn't pretend objectivity you don't have yet.

3. **Centralize the register.** Move from "EOXS Salary Details" + "EOXS Payroll" + scattered xlsx files → **one canonical `EOXS-Payroll-Master.xlsx`** with tabs:
   - Employee roster (name, level, department, DOJ, status)
   - Monthly payroll 2023–current (one row per employee per month)
   - Bonus history (who, when, reason, amount)
   - Raise log (who, when, old salary, new salary, reason)

4. **Bring interns/VAs into the register.** Those 24 people need rows, even if unpaid initially. Tag them as "visible" so they don't get batch-rate errors.

---

### **Tier 2: Decision (by end of Q4 2026)**

Decide **how much structure you want**, then operationalize it.

**Question 1: Do you want formula-based pay, or discretionary with transparent criteria?**

| Approach | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| **Formula** (Level → Base + Bonus % + Accrual) | Scales; defensible; fair. | Rigid; hard to adjust for individuals; requires clear levels. |
| **Discretionary + transparent** (publish ranges + reason each raise) | Flexible; keeps you in control; rewards outcomes not just level. | Requires you to document every decision; people still compare. |
| **Hybrid** (L0–L3 formula, L4+ discretionary) | Sweet spot for a 50-person company. | Needs two systems; more admin. |

**Recommendation:** **Hybrid.** L0–L3 by formula (you have precedent), L4+ you decide case-by-case. Redo this in 18 months when you're bigger.

**Question 2: How do you handle the 2026 contraction?**

Current state: pay dropped ~RESTRICTED. That's done; you can't undo it. Going forward:

- **Restoration raises are defensible** (Hashir's case: "Brought back to Sep 2025 level" = ✅).
- **New raises need explicit reason** (Dhrup: "Waiting for communication/productivity clarity" = ✅ reason to hold).
- **Don't raise anyone on a weaker signal than whoever else is waiting** (precedent risk).

**Action:** Review every open raise case (Hashir, Dhrup, others) side-by-side. Decide: restore, deny, or defer. Document the reason *once* — it becomes your reference for the next five asks.

**Question 3: Does AskCruz get separate compensation?**

You documented: "No separate entity, no separate payroll."  
You practice: Company column "Cruz" in the register, separate letterhead, separate addresses, separate hiring.

**Pick one:** Either it's a product (all AskCruz people are EOXS employees at EOXS rates), or it's a subsidiary (separate budget, possibly separate rates later). Document that choice. The current state—separate in practice, unified on paper—is the root of the Nandita Rana error.

---

### **Tier 3: Scale (2027 Onward)**

Once Tiers 1–2 are live and Q4 2026 hiring starts:

1. **Publish the levels.** Not secret, but internal. People know where they are and where they can go.
2. **Add a "promotion track" document.** What does L1 → L2 need? (tenure, output, role complexity, peer review?). Make it real.
3. **Tie bonuses to metrics.** Revenue, project delivery, customer retention—pick 2–3 and pay them consistently.
4. **Annual reviews + raise cycles.** Right now it's ad-hoc. Move to calendar-based (say, April) so people know when to ask.
