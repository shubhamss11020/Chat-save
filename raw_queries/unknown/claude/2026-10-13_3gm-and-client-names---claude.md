What is 3gm?

## Claude (2026-10-13 10:34:51 EDT)

3GM Steel, Inc. (legal name on documents: “Three G M Steel, Inc.”) is a Somerset, Kentucky steel trading and distribution company. It buys coil from mills and importers, holds inventory at ports and warehouses, and sells to customers such as carport and metal-building makers. This comes from the vault wiki only; I didn’t check the ERP because the question doesn’t need a live figure.

Business: It deals in coated and painted coil (PBR, standing seam, RMP and similar products). It is a C-corp. Its accounting is compiled on an income-tax basis by an outside CPA firm.
Suppliers and customers: Mill and import suppliers include SeAH Steel America, Dongkuk and Big River Steel. Customers include Carolina Carports and Real Steel.
Group structure: It is part of a family of companies with Mac Metal Sales, Inc. 3GM sells to Mac Metal (intercompany sales) and places some buys jointly with it, such as the Q1 2027 Dongkuk order.
People: Stefan Brown is CFO and Travis Lane is Chief Commercial Officer. Five sales reps are Adam Buck, Jack Hall, Maggie Moore, Matt Inman and Tadd Sapone. Jessica Worley is Director of Supply Chain.
Scale: Year-end 2025 sales were $152.4M, from the CPA’s compilation. That statement is unaudited and unreviewed, and the accountants state they are not independent of 3GM.
Current activity: 3GM is the buyer in the CMT Components acquisition. The August 2026 executive summary puts the price at about $18mm.

The vault flags two of 3GM’s own documents as disagreeing on Jan–Aug 2026 gross profit: a QuickBooks export shows a loss and the sales report shows a profit. I haven’t verified either, so check the ERP before quoting year-to-date profit.
