Where my “adjacent to steel proof” claim is weak

Proof is one client (3GM), still mid-implementation, with no measured results. A prospect in a neighbouring industry will not be persuaded by that. The real advantage is that you can reuse the pitch and existing contacts, not that the product is proven there.

Is this helpful for us?

It is useful as a starting hypothesis and not yet as a decision. I haven’t checked the three things that decide fit:

Company size: are most of these firms roughly 20 to 500 employees, or mostly tiny?
Reachability: can you find the CEO or owner, and are they active on LinkedIn?
Tool stack: do they run on Gmail or Google Workspace and systems AskCruz can connect to?
My ranking, given what I know
Industrial machinery wholesalers: largest list, closest buyer profile to steel distributors.
Industrial supplies wholesalers: same profile, smaller list.
Metal fabricators: I’d keep it only if most firms exceed about 20 employees.
Contractors: I’d drop it unless you can show a reason.
A fast way to replace my guesses with evidence

For each industry, pull about 50 random companies from your data provider and check:

Employee count (what share fall in your target band)
Whether the CEO or owner is findable on LinkedIn
Website or job-post clues about their tools (Google Workspace, Odoo, a named ERP)
Whether the owner is active on LinkedIn

About two hours of work per industry would give you real percentages. Industries where fewer than about a third pass the size and CEO checks drop out. That tells you more than any reasoning from me.

I’ll look for evidence on these industries’ data problems so the reasons aren’t only my reasoning by analogy.

Reasons these industries can be chosen

Each reason carries a tag so you can see how much weight it can bear:

[Internal] comes from your AskCruz or EOXS documents.
[Sourced] comes from an outside source I found. Most are vendor blogs that quote surveys, so check the original before using a figure externally.
[Reasoned] is my inference, not verified.
Reasons that apply to all of them
They match AskCruz’s own ICP. [Internal] AskCruz defines its target by fragmented data across several systems, not by industry. Its documented buyer is the customer’s CEO.
They match the buyer profile of your steel list. [Internal] The EOXS ICP targets owners, presidents, CEOs and operations heads at distributors with roughly 10 to 200 employees. Wholesalers have the same structure, so the same outreach and titles work.
You can reuse the story. [Reasoned] These industries are neighbours of steel distribution, so the pitch, case study (3GM) and contacts carry over. This is a cost advantage, not proof the product works there.
